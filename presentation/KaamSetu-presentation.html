    Payments & revenue

    How payment works.

    Agree

    Farmer and labourer agree on the wage through offers and bids.

    On booking

    Farmer accepts and pays wage + service charge into the KaamSetu wallet.

    Farmer cancels

    Amount refunded, service charge deducted.

    Labourer cancels

    Labourer's wallet goes minus by the service charge.
